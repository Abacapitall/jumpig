Jumpig is an endless vertical platform challenge where you control Piggy, jump from Ground to Ground, avoid dangerous traps, and push your score as high as possible.
There is no final floor.
How far can you climb?

The Challenge

Control Piggy, move left and right, and use your single jump carefully to reach higher Grounds.
While in the air, you can still control Piggy's horizontal movement.
Watch the traps ahead, react at the right moment, choose your route when a choice is available, and try to survive as long as possible.
Every run is a challenge for your Best Score.
