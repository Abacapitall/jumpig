JUMPIG PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

Version: 1.0

Developer / Publisher: Nguyen Xuan Truong / Aba Games

Game: Jumpig

1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy explains how Jumpig handles information when you download, install, or play the game.

Jumpig is an offline-first gameplay experience. The core game does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Jumpig does, however, use third-party services for advertising and in-app purchases. Those services may process certain information from your device as described below.

2. INFORMATION STORED BY JUMPIG

Jumpig stores gameplay and game-state information needed to operate the game. This may include:

  • Best Score
  • Free Lives
  • Purchased Lives
  • Remove Ads entitlement
  • Game Over / cooldown state
  • Other gameplay state required to restore the game correctly

    • Gameplay save information is stored locally on the device by the game.

    Jumpig does not require an online account for the core gameplay.

    3. ADVERTISING

    Jumpig uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide advertisements, including banner advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements.

    The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as IP address, user interactions with the app or advertisements, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, depending on the SDK configuration and the services used.

    This information may be used by the advertising provider for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and related service purposes.

    Jumpig does not sell your personal information.

    The handling of information by Google Mobile Ads is also subject to Google's applicable policies and terms.

    4. IN-APP PURCHASES

    Jumpig may offer optional in-app purchases through the applicable app-store payment system.

    The planned Jumpig purchase products are:

  • 10 Purchased Lives — USD 0.99
  • Remove Ads — USD 1.99

    • Payments are processed by the applicable app store. Jumpig does not directly receive or store your payment card number or other full payment credentials.

    Purchase and transaction information may be processed by the applicable platform according to its privacy policy and terms.

    5. INFORMATION WE DO NOT REQUEST FOR CORE GAMEPLAY

    Jumpig does not require the player to provide:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Precise location
  • An account or login

    • If a future version introduces a feature that requires additional information, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

    6. DATA SHARING

    Jumpig does not sell personal information.

    Information may be transmitted to third-party service providers when required to provide advertising, process in-app purchases, prevent fraud, maintain security, or operate platform services.

    Third-party providers process information under their own applicable privacy policies and terms.

    7. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

    Gameplay data stored locally by Jumpig remains on the device until it is overwritten, reset, deleted by the game, or removed by the user through device or app-management actions.

    Jumpig does not maintain a player account database for core gameplay.

    Data handled by third-party advertising or app-store services is retained and deleted according to the applicable provider's policies and systems.

    8. SECURITY

    Jumpig and its third-party services use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the services involved.

    Information transmitted by supported third-party services may be protected in transit using encryption such as TLS.

    No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

    9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

    Jumpig is not designed to require children to submit personal information for core gameplay.

    The developer will comply with applicable requirements concerning children and advertising in the jurisdictions where Jumpig is distributed.

    Because advertising services may process device or advertising information, the final store configuration and age/audience settings must be kept consistent with the applicable platform and advertising-provider requirements.

    10. YOUR CHOICES

    You may stop using Jumpig at any time by uninstalling the game.

    You may also control certain device-level advertising and privacy settings through your operating system or app-store settings, where such controls are available.

    11. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

    Jumpig uses third-party services that may process information independently of the developer.

    Relevant services include:

  • Google Mobile Ads for advertising
  • Google Play / Apple App Store services for distribution and in-app purchases

    • Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

    12. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

    This Privacy Policy may be updated when Jumpig, its features, SDKs, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

    The latest version will be made available through the public Jumpig Privacy Policy webpage and, where appropriate, inside the game.
