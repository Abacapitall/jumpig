1. INTRODUCTION
This Privacy Policy explains how Jumpig handles information when you use the game.
Jumpig is developed and published by Nguyen Xuan Truong / Aba Games.
Jumpig is designed to provide an endless vertical platform challenge. The core gameplay does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
Jumpig itself may store gameplay and game-state information needed to operate the game, such as:
• Best Score
• Free Lives
• Referral Lives
• Purchased Lives
• Game Over cooldown information
• Save version and related game-state information
This information may be stored locally on your device through the game's save system.
Jumpig does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, or precise location for core gameplay.
3. ADVERTISING AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
Jumpig may display advertisements, including banner advertisements and rewarded advertisements.
Advertising services may collect or process certain information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws. The information handled by a third-party advertising provider may depend on the SDK configuration, platform, consent settings, and the services enabled in the released version of Jumpig.
Before release, the developer should verify the final advertising SDK configuration and provide the applicable third-party privacy information.
4. PURCHASES
Jumpig may offer optional in-app purchases, including gameplay benefits and an option to remove advertisements.
Payments are processed through the applicable app store or platform payment system. Jumpig does not directly process your payment card details.
Purchase-related information may be handled by the relevant platform and services according to their policies.
5. REFERRAL FEATURES
Jumpig may provide referral features that allow players to invite other users and receive eligible in-game rewards.
Referral data and verification may depend on the final implementation and services used by the released version of the game.
The developer should verify the final referral implementation before publishing this Privacy Policy.
6. DATA STORAGE AND RETENTION
Local gameplay data may remain on your device until it is deleted, reset, or otherwise removed according to the game's behavior and the platform's storage rules.
Information processed by third-party services is retained according to the applicable third-party service policies.
7. DATA SHARING
Jumpig does not sell your personal information.
Information may be processed by third-party services that are necessary for advertising, purchases, platform functionality, or other services enabled in the released version of the game.
The developer should review all enabled third-party SDKs and services before publication to ensure that this section accurately describes actual data sharing.
8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Jumpig is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
The developer will take reasonable steps to comply with applicable children's privacy requirements in the regions where Jumpig is distributed.
9. SECURITY
The developer takes reasonable measures to protect information handled by Jumpig and its enabled services.
However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
10. YOUR CHOICES
You may stop using Jumpig at any time.
You may also be able to control certain advertising, privacy, or platform permissions through your device or app-store settings, depending on the services enabled in the game.
For requests concerning information handled by third-party services, you may need to contact the relevant service provider directly.
11. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY
This Privacy Policy may be updated when Jumpig, its features, or its third-party services change.
The latest version should be made available through the official Jumpig privacy policy webpage and, where appropriate, within the game.
