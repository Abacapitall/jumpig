HOW TO PLAY

PLAY CONTROL

Control Piggy left and right to move on the Ground.
Jump onto the next Ground to progress further and earn a higher score.
Piggy can only jump once to reach the Grounds above.

CONTROL PIGGY IN THE AIR

Piggy can still move left and right while jumping upward.
Piggy can also move left and right while falling downward.
Use this air control to adjust your position and land safely.

AVOID THE TRAPS

Traps are placed along your path.
Watch ahead, recognize the danger, and react at the right moment.
Use movement and your single jump to avoid the traps and stay alive.

CHOOSE A SAFE ROUTE

Some situations may give you more than one route.
Look carefully and choose the safer path when a choice is available.
Your decision can determine whether Piggy survives.

TIMING & POSITION

Good timing and positioning are important.
Do not jump too early or too late.
Move Piggy into the right position before and during the jump.
Small movements can make the difference between landing safely and falling.

WHEN YOU DIE

If Piggy hits a lethal trap or falls from a playable Ground, the run ends for that attempt.
A Life is consumed.
If you still have a usable Life, you can continue the run.
If no usable Life remains, the run goes to Game Over.

GET THE HIGHEST SCORE

Jump higher, progress further, survive longer, and pass traps to increase your score.
Your goal is not to finish the game.
Your goal is to achieve the highest score possible and beat your Best Score.

THIS IS JUMPIG

Jumpig is an endless challenge.
There is no final floor.
Every jump matters.
Every decision matters.
How far can you climb?

SHARE YOUR SCORE

Beat your Best Score and share your score with your friends.
Challenge them to climb higher than you.

KEEP CLIMBING

Stay focused.
Watch the traps.
Control Piggy carefully.
Jump once.
Keep climbing.
